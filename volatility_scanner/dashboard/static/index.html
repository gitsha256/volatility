Data quality & calculation definitions +

PCR/time scores

PCR / t, PCR / t² and PCR / t³ use ACT/365 calendar years. They are time-weighted scores, not mathematical velocity, acceleration or jerk. Actual derivatives are separate.

Live versus recorded

Live calculations refresh every second. Historical algorithm points are recorded every 60 seconds. Each chart updates its current candle and metric point in place; unavailable points break the line.

Observations versus models

Premium residuals need fresh receipt and trade timestamps. Dealer signs and IV/Charm shocks are explicit scenarios. Closed markets, expired contracts and missing IV are labeled.

Window and freshness

A five-minute window contains about 301 one-second samples. A gap over 2.5 seconds resets warmup. OI receipt ages over 15 seconds are withheld. First captured candles may be partial.
